# Disclaimer

Cover_Maker is a local audio tool that separates audio files into stems and
renders practice mixes. This document is not legal advice.

## Use only audio you are allowed to process

Only process audio you own or are entitled to use: your own recordings,
DRM-free purchases, copies of your own physical media where your local law
allows it, or content released under a licence that permits it (for example
Creative Commons). Cover_Maker does not download audio from streaming platforms
and must not be used to circumvent technical protection measures.

## Publishing results

Separating a recording into stems does not remove the rights attached to it.
A backing track made from an original commercial recording generally requires
permission from the owners of both the recording and the composition before it
can be published or shared. Covers you re-record yourself may still require a
licence for the composition. Check the rules of the platform where you publish.

## AI models

Cover_Maker does not include or host any AI model weights. Models are downloaded
from their original sources only after you review and accept their licence.
Many community models have no explicit licence, a non-commercial licence or a
copyleft licence; you are responsible for complying with those terms.
